The perfect pizza recipe:

Ingredients (1 large pizza)

Dough

2¼ tsp (1 packet) active dry yeast

1 tsp sugar

¾ cup warm water

2 cups all-purpose flour

1 tsp salt

1 tbsp olive oil

Toppings

½ cup pizza sauce

1–1½ cups shredded mozzarella

Toppings of choice

Olive oil (for brushing)

Instructions

Activate yeast: Mix warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let sit 5–10 minutes until foamy.

Make dough: Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Mix, then knead 5–8 minutes until smooth.

Rise: Cover and let rise 45–60 minutes until doubled.

Preheat oven: 475°F (245°C). Preheat a pizza stone or baking sheet.

Shape: Roll/stretch dough into a 12-inch circle.

Top: Spread sauce, add cheese and toppings. Lightly brush crust with olive oil.

Bake: 12–15 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.

Cool slightly, slice, enjoy
